# The Hollow Wraith

*Check-in is permanent.*

**The Hollow Wraith** is a first-person horror game that runs entirely in your web browser. You wake up in the foyer of an abandoned manor, half of it rotting old house and half of it a decaying hotel. The front door is locked, and you are not alone.

Somewhere in the dark, the Hollow Wraith drifts through the corridors: a hooded, skull-masked creature with long hooked claws and a serpent's tail, hovering above the floorboards. It wanders, it listens, and the moment it sees you, it runs.

To escape, you have to search the house for the keys to the front door. They're hidden in dusty bedrooms, a crumbling library, a nursery and dozens of numbered hotel rooms, some lying on the floor and some tucked away in drawers. Find four of them, fit each one into the locks of the front door, and get out before the Wraith finds you.

## Features

- **Two floors to explore:** a ground-floor manor of bedrooms, a library, a kitchen and a grand hall, and an upstairs hotel with over fifty numbered guest rooms, connected by a grand staircase.
- **A hunter that sees and remembers:** the Wraith only sees what's in front of it, loses you around corners, runs to where it last saw you, and searches before giving up.
- **Hide or run:** sprint with limited stamina, or slip into a louvred cupboard and watch it pass through the slats.
- **Atmosphere first:** flickering wall lamps, a failing flashlight, lightning through the windows, peeling wallpaper, creaking doors and a presence you can hear before you see it.
- **Your kind of nightmare:** choose from 1 to 10 Wraiths and Easy, Medium or Hard difficulty, which changes how fast they run and how many keys are hidden.
- **No installation:** everything, from textures to the house itself, is generated in code. Just open the file and play.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox) and click **Enter**. Headphones are strongly recommended.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `W` `A` `S` `D` | Move |
| Mouse | Look around |
| `Shift` | Run (uses stamina) |
| `C` | Crouch |
| `E` | Open doors and drawers, pick up keys, hide in cupboards |
| `F` | Flashlight |
| `P` | FPS counter |
| `Esc` | Pause |
